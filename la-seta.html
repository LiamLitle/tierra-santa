La Seta — Junglekitchen

Named after the mystical mushroom.

La Seta — the ultimate connector of the earth — is an ultra-local culinary experience. The kitchen sits a few meters from our organic garden and elevates traditional Costa Rican flavors into Ayurveda-inspired fine dining. The menu doesn't follow trends: it's a fixed selection of signature dishes, prepared with ingredients of absolute freshness.

Entrées

  • The Ritual — Mini Tortillas Aliñadas: traditional corn tortillas with fresh garden herbs, hand-whipped botanical butter.
  • The Soul — "La Seta" Mushroom Essence: a deep umami broth poured over pan-seared jungle mushrooms and a sacred basil leaf.
  • The Geometry — The Ratatouille Papaya Salad: layers of green papaya, sweet orange papaya and aged parmesan, lime-honey emulsion and crushed red chili.

Plats

  • Mahi-Mahi "Golfo Dulce" — crispy fillet, local chayote-carrot picadillo, lemongrass-infused rice.
  • Pollo Encebollado Extremo — slow-braised chicken, tomato-chili reduction, oven-baked yuca-coconut cakes and plantain chips.
  • Beef Filet "Chicharrones Style" — fried beef fillet pieces, spiced corn breading, jungle herb and red pepper chimichurri, yuca-coconut cakes.

Dessert

  • The Divine Abundance — Tres Leches Rice Pudding with a fresh mango-papaya stew and dark chocolate ice cream made with indigenous cacao.

All dinners are served in the open jungle pavilion, by candlelight. Reservation required.
