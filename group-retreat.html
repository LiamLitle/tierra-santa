The Circle

Around the fire, as our ancestors did.

We work with the same sacred elements as our individual healing container, yet here the focus is on group energy. We lift each other by honouring our personal stories and sharing the threads of our lives. The sacred tree stands at the heart of the circle; the serpent way teaches us to shed what no longer serves; fire and the untamed spirit of the Osa hold every step.

What we work with

Sixteen doorways, one fire.

Our days are woven from the practices below — held in the energy of the group, with the finca itself as co-facilitator.

Please understand that the sequence of the individual components depends on my personal decisions during the process, in order to capture the magic of the moment and honor the ongoing interaction with the spirits of the ancestors and nature.

Silent sitting that brings the body and mind back to the breath of the forest.
