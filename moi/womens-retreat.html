Women's RetreatLa Cara

A circle of women gathered in the lush, fertile embrace of the Osa rainforest — where the same deep elements as our individual healing container are held in group energy.

Reserve Your Place Discover the Circle
The Circle

When women gather, the forest listens.

We work with the same sacred elements as our individual healing container, yet here the focus is on group energy. Together we honour the elements through ceremony, shamanic drumming and singing that stirs the blood, and dances that open the body to remember what the mind has forgotten.

You walk with indigenous women through the living jungle, learning from those who have listened to the forest for generations.

What we work with

Sixteen doorways, one circle.

Our days are woven from the practices below — held in the energy of the group, where each woman's presence lifts the others.

Please understand that the sequence of the individual components depends on my personal decisions during the process, in order to capture the magic of the moment and honor the ongoing interaction with the spirits of the ancestors and nature.
