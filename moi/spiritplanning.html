Excursions to Ancient Indigenous Sites

As part of the seminar, we visit sacred places of Costa Rica's indigenous heritage — living field studies that connect the principles of SpiritPlanning® with the wisdom of the land. Among them: an ancient gateway aligned with the Orion formation, and Finca 6, a UNESCO World Heritage site home to the mysterious pre-Columbian stone spheres of the Diquís culture.

Learning objective: Experience the dialogue between ancestral geomancy, cosmic alignment and the principles you are learning.
