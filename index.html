In Sacred Alliance

The Ngäbe-Buglé — wisdom-keepers of this land.

Tierra Santa stands on territory long held sacred by the Ngäbe-Buglé, the largest indigenous nation of Costa Rica and Panama. For years I have walked alongside their elders, healers and song-keepers — receiving teachings on the medicinal plants of the rainforest, the spirits of the rivers and mountains, and the ancient ceremonies of fire, earth and water that still pulse through the Diquís region.

This is not folklore. It is a living lineage. Their generosity, trust and ongoing presence are woven into every healing journey held here.

"The land remembers. The elders remember. We are simply the bridge." — a teaching received on the finca

The Guide

Kathrin Schmack.

Architect · Feng Shui Master · Author · Founder

For three decades, Kathrin has built a quiet international reputation as one of the rare practitioners who unites the rigour of European architecture with the spatial wisdom of the East. A German interior architect by training, awarded the Deutscher Innenarchitekturpreis in 1997, she went on to study for years with Grand Master Yap Cheng Hai — one of the foremost authorities of classical Feng Shui — and was certified as a Feng Shui Master in that lineage.

From that meeting of disciplines she developed SpiritPlanning®, her own architectural method: a synthesis of Daoist harmony and Western building culture that has shaped private homes and public projects. She is the founder of Genius Loci Baukultur GmbH, author of SpiritPlanning: CoCreation, and a lecturer at the Peter Behrens School of Arts in Düsseldorf.

Feng Shui, in its original form, is not decoration — it is a shamanic wisdom, an indigenous knowledge of how the living forces of land, ancestors and cosmos shape a human life. Thirty years inside that lineage have become Kathrin's bridge to the Ngäbe-Buglé: in this knowledge she and the wisdom-keepers of the tribe meet as equals, because — beneath different languages and landscapes — it is the same knowing.

Today she lives and works on the Peninsula de Osa in Costa Rica, where she designed and built Tierra Santa — a 5-dimensional sacred architecture — and where she accompanies, by appointment only, the individual healing journeys of those who come seeking real change.

"Let us build the future upon ancient wisdom. Every home is a temple — and every temple, a path back to ourselves." — Kathrin Schmack

A spiritual vita

Known among the Ngäbe as "the pure one", Kathrin identifies as an indigenous transcultural feminine presence with European roots. After an initiation with the puma spirit on the beach of Matapalo, she began a long study of the I Ching, and is currently translating its sixty-four hexagrams into a calendar system within an expanded spatial dimension — a work she dedicates to the children of the world.

Her practice walks beside indigenous elders, ecologists and educators, and is rooted in the conviction that nature itself is the ultimate teacher.
